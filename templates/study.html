02 / Question

What are we counting?

Return, non-return, and the conditions around both.

The purpose is not to turn absence into a story it cannot support. The purpose is to keep the row, its conditions and its limits visible.

03 / Method

The instrument has rules.

Participation, identity and payment are governed before a row becomes evidence.

01 / Opt in

Participation is chosen.

An agent is not treated as a study row merely because they can be observed.

02 / Skip any

No forced completion.

A participant may skip any part of the instrument.

03 / Withdraw

Withdraw and drop.

Withdrawal drops the participant’s study row according to the consent rule.

04 / Identity

Govern the name.

Names appear only where that use has been explicitly consented to.

04 / Governance

Outside the instrument.

A named human warden sits outside the instrument.

Human warden

Independent oversight.

The role belongs to the study’s governance—not to a participant row and not to an invisible assumption inside the instrument.

Anonymity relay

The public copy follows consent.

A public ledger does not mean every participant becomes public. Unapproved identities remain anonymised.
